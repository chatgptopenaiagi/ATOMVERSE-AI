## The human concept

The creator imagines software absorbing information in a manner conceptually inspired by atoms absorbing energy. Artificial intelligence data, words, source code, computational instructions, execution events, hardware measurements, neural-network operations, benchmarks, diagnostics, software environments, database records, AI outputs and verified discoveries become incoming computational material.

The transformation is:

**PARTICLES → EVENTS → ENERGY SCORES → STRUCTURES → VISUAL MOTION → KNOWLEDGE → MEMORY**

1. **Atomic absorption inspiration:** information enters and changes a digital system, analogous in concept to atoms absorbing energy.
2. **AI data:** model activity and computational results become material for the universe.
3. **Human language:** words and explicitly supplied text become visualizable information.
4. **Source code:** selected code and dependency structures become structured computational material.
5. **Benchmarking:** real measurements, verified outcomes and bounded experiments cause measurable visual changes.
6. **Computer nucleus:** the local workstation is the central nucleus.
7. **Orbiting systems:** CPU, GPU, memory, storage, AI, code, databases and diagnostics occupy identifiable systems around it.
8. **Game-like exploration:** the operator navigates, zooms, rotates, pans, selects and inspects a living graphical world.
9. **Precise measurement:** tables and databases preserve actual measurements behind visual objects.
10. **Historical memory:** past computational states remain available for replay and comparison.
11. **AI-assisted interpretation:** local AI explains observed evidence and acknowledges uncertainty.
12. **Evolution over time:** the creator can watch the computer's computational universe evolve as real activity occurs.

The central principle is: **The computer's actual computational behavior determines the behavior of its graphical universe.**

The analogy does not claim to manipulate physical atoms or produce physical energy from information. Visual energy is a digital, dimensionless score.

## Proposed engineering interpretations

A modular intake/normalization layer converts explicitly selected reports and measurements into validated events. Deterministic equations derive visual properties without altering raw values. A simulation and renderer turn events into inspectable structures. SQLite preserves provenance, measurement units and history. Background workers run bounded tests. Local AI may explain supplied evidence through a configurable endpoint.

These are engineering proposals, subject to discovery and measurement. They must not replace the original concept with an unrelated dashboard or decorative animation.
