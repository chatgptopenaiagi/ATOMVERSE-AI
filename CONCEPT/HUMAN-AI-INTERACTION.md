# Human and AI interaction

The operator chooses sources and controls ingestion, replay, rendering and benchmarks. Active sources and synthetic/real modes are always visible.

Questions such as 'Why is my GPU orbit red?' should cite recorded errors, configuration and evidence. AI output is an interpretation, not an observation. A local endpoint is configurable; unavailable models produce an honest unavailable state.

Imported code, reports and model output are untrusted data. No model output may run commands or claim a repair without a separately authorized action and verified result. Do not intercept private Codex sessions.
