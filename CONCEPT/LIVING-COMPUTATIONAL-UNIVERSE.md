# Living computational universe

The workstation is a central nucleus. CPU, GPU, RAM/VRAM, storage, AI, code, database and Guardian systems occupy separate orbits. A historical field makes previous states explorable.

Real-data mode is driven by measured or explicitly imported events. Animation may interpolate between states but must not invent measurements. Idle visual motion is an artistic property, not evidence of workload. Selection links every data particle to its normalized event and available evidence.

Demonstration mode is self-contained and clearly marked DEMO: SYNTHETIC DATA. It never silently fills gaps in real mode.
