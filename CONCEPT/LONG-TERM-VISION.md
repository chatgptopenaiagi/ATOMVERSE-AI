# Long-term vision

ATOMVERSE may grow into an explorable record of computational behavior: hardware activity, AI execution, software relationships and historical engineering discoveries.

Regression detection requires comparable hardware, environment, workload, warmup, synchronization and versions. Changes without comparable conditions are differences, not proven improvements.

Future work includes larger instanced universes, richer knowledge graphs, code dependency views, evidence-grounded local AI and optional analytics engines. None is promised as implemented at genesis.
