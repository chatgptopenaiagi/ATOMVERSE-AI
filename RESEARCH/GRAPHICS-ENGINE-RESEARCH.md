# Graphics engine research

Official capabilities and local availability were compared for Godot, Three.js, Babylon.js, Bevy and native/Python renderers. The alpha uses Qt software painting of a mathematical 3D projection to reuse existing tools and preserve GPU compute headroom. Godot 4.7.2 is installed but not coupled to the alpha. See [evaluation](ENGINE-EVALUATION.md), [visual specification](../ATOMVERSE-VISUALIZATION-SPEC.md) and actual-window screenshots under EVIDENCE. This is not a full 3D physics engine or instanced GPU renderer.
